# Why Scrip stops pulling you back: the four explorations in one page

Sources: `explore-run.md`, `explore-meta.md`, `explore-craft-trade.md`,
`explore-engagement.md` (each with evidence, 11-12 options, costs and cheap
tests). No mechanics were changed.

## What all four found, independently

1. **You never get to feel broken.** Late enemy health rises 1.0 -> 2.1x to
   cancel build growth: one-hit kills fall from ~60% to ~40% between waves 4
   and 11, enemies live longer as the run goes on, and the late screen looks
   like wave 3. Only the sling stack escapes. (run, engagement)
2. **The build is finished by wave 5-8, then nothing.** Six weapon slots full
   by wave 5; 650-950 unspent scrip at benches 9-11; real bench choices fall
   to ~1% by bench 10. (run, engagement)
3. **Builds are numbers, not rules.** Three attack shapes, 57 of 80 items are
   stats, everything stacks additively, fusion only reshuffles parents
   (96% of results are the parents' effects added up), and the only
   rule-breakers (relics) are ~2,000 hours away. (run, craft)
4. **Wants run out at run ~10 (hour 1.5-2.5).** Every scheduled unlock is
   done; wins and hazard climbs give only marks and kit room; the next goals
   are 2,415 fusion pairs or a relic. (meta, engagement)
5. **Nothing to aim at between runs.** Fusion is blind guessing with a known
   strength; marks buy only upkeep; other players appear as counts, not
   events; the town never sends you back into a run for something. (craft,
   meta)
6. **No surprises.** No drops, chests or rare finds in the arena; the same
   elites on the same waves every run. (run, engagement)

## The shortlist (ranked by how many reports reach it, then by cost)

| # | Direction | Answers | Cost | Cheapest test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Let runs get broken**: flatten late enemy health, add a few multiplying breakpoint items; move difficulty into elites, telegraphs and hazard | 1, 3 | M | Sim: late one-hit share >= 70%, boss kill time spread, win rate 55-65% |
| 2 | **Masterworks**: a tier-4 weapon plus a specific item becomes an evolved weapon with a new rule, found through the world recipe book, first finder named | 2, 3, 5 | M | Hand-author 4 evolutions, sim their win rates |
| 3 | **Wins unlock content on the character x hazard ladder** (Brotato's long tail), plus per-character tracks that move on losses and deeds ("hold 4 bows -> Bowyer") | 4 | M | List 36 cells and what each unlocks on paper |
| 4 | **Always show the next goal, close**: nearest unlock and distance on every recap and home page; title rungs; fusion pairs in chapters of 20-40 | 4 | S | Add the line to the homecoming only |
| 5 | **Known targets for crafting**: a daily board of 3 commissions and a seasonal book of target items as silhouettes; fusion's luck moves to the item's identity, not its strength | 5, 3 | M | 3 hand-written commissions, 10 targets |
| 6 | **Arena surprises**: elite chests opened at the bench with run-only rule-breakers; shuffled elites; per-run conditions | 2, 6 | M | One chest type on elites, sim it |
| 7 | **Make power visible**: damage numbers that grow, weapon sprites by tier, denser late hordes, a level-up moment, a damage recap and personal best on the end screen | 1 | S-M | Bigger numbers and the recap first |
| 8 | **A cabin and town contracts**: things marks buy that you can see; contracts that send you into a run for a specific item or feat; royalties when your recipe is used | 5 | M-L | Three contracts as a list |

From the New World comparison (owner's question, 2026-11-12): trade-skill
levels with XP every run, rare finds inside common nodes, refining tiers that
projects need, consumables packed and used up, and a town that is a place.
These slot under 5 and 8.

## Suggested order

Start with **1 + 7** (the run feels like Brotato within a run), then **4**
(cheap, fixes the hour-2 cliff), then **2 + 5** (crafting gets targets and
the run gets its "click"), then **3**, **6**, **8**.
