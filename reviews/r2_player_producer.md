# Round 2 review: veteran player and producer

## 1. Verdict

Version 2 is a better document and still not a game I would be playing in month 3: it moved the overload from the wave to the pre-run screen, and its own simulation still says gate-1 farming beats deep runs for every skill level. As a producer I count 87 nodes where there were 75, with nothing from the "never" bucket actually cut.

## 3. Attention recount

**In a wave.** The six: position, health, stamina, one cooldown, heat, beat. Other nodes add: second weapon cooldown and which weapon is active; marked rich-node trip and its lock timer; three to four enemy roles; burrowers on nodes; the wave objective; the shrine price in force; the weekly mutator; perfect-strike timing; a rival ghost on marked waves. Weight as movement speed is a fair exemption. Honest count: about 12. The budget node is a wish; `wave-objectives`, `rival-ghost-builds`, `weekly-mutators` and the two-weapon ability node break it and none was edited to comply.

**At camp.** Three ordinary decisions, but "one shop action (buy or merge)" guts the Brotato shop (I buy three things and merge twice per shop), and automatic level-ups delete a choice players like. At a boss gate: shop, craft, route, wild pick, evolution, push-or-extract. Six.

## 4. New problems

- **Need picks the weapon, the weapon picks the route.** Harvest type is fixed by weapon family, persistent gear fixes the two families, and routes are "good or bad for certain weapon types". The town project decides the run before it starts. Every best rule in the sim has route = need.
- **The pre-run screen is the new overload.** Kit, two weapon families, four tools, charms, bag, eight recipes, key and biome, oils, flasks, patterns, mutator check: about ten decisions before the first kill. Brotato has three.
- **The breakout cannot be delivered.** Evolution needs a boss chest, so it lands at wave 8 at the earliest, in the segment where the bag is fullest, the player slowest and extraction most rational. Best play averages 10 to 11 of 12 waves.
- **Camp craft is busywork.** You always hold the bundle because your weapon produces it; "always craft" costs 1-7%.
- **Deep-only materials punish the casual.** They die 36% at best play and upper tiers need segment 3. It is a gate, not a reason; the tuning log admits depth and farming "pulled against each other in every set tried".
- **Kits contradict rules** ("crafts at every camp" is already everyone's rule).

## 5. Re-walk

**Hour 5.** Good. Teaching order works, heat bites, first evolution lands. *Quit moment:* first death in segment 3 with the schematic I pushed for.

**Hour 50.** I pick a weapon from the town board, slot the same eight recipes, craft at every camp, farm gate 1 when I need commons. Kits are the only novelty. *Quit moment:* realising the loadout screen takes longer than the first two waves.

**Month 3.** Codex near done, season adds a tier. No market, so segment-3 parts I cannot farm are simply missing. *Quit moment:* the reset, as before.

## 6. Scope

Differently large, and larger: 75 nodes to 87, "keep" from 38 to 56, zero moved to "never". Riskiest node now: `depth-pays-in-things-that-farming-the-first-boss-cannot-give`. It multiplies content (segments x route kinds x weapon harvest families x schematic lists) to paper over an economic conflict the author's own tuning could not resolve. Runner-up: twenty-plus kits. The market remains fatal the day it is un-staged.

## 7. Tonight

Brotato. Version 2's 25 minutes now match it, but Brotato's shop lets me buy what I find, not what my town board ordered. For mining under fire, Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor still does it with zero pre-run homework.

## 8. Convergence

**Blockers**
- Gate-1 farming out-earns depth; fix the payout, not the gate.
- Need-weapon-route chain makes the run a foregone conclusion.
- The six-thing budget is not enforced on the nodes that break it.
- Pre-run loadout has no budget of its own.

**Major**
- Breakout segment unreachable or irrational.
- Camp craft and shrine are non-choices for two of three skill levels.
- One shop action per camp.
- No solo source for deep materials.
- "Never" list still deferred, not cut; options-not-stats still contradicted.

**Minor**
- Stale thief, bag-down, perfect-strike and weekly-cap text; duplicate bag nodes; "discovered by trying"; three collections.

**Needs a prototype, not more paper**
Whether gathering under fire is fun at all; whether heat reads at a glance; whether 12 tracked things feel like 6; stamina shared between dodge, heavy swing and mining; every feel node. The sim has been tuned 13 times and passes 1 to 8 of 8 depending on the check: it has nothing left to say. Stop revising and build the five-wave arena.
